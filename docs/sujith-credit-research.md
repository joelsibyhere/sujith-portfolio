Prefer official soundtrack descriptions, album booklets and film end credits. A song-level credit establishes participation in that song, not every track, the background score or the theatrical final mix. Where public sources disagree, preserve the uncertainty and obtain the client's credit record.

The inventory includes 59 artworks, of which 13 now match local website projects. The remaining 46 are candidates for the next content pass. It would be misleading to equate either the website's 25 entries or the poster count with a researched complete 600+ archive.
