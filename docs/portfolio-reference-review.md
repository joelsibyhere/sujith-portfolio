## Recommended direction

Keep the agreed pastel sage background, ivory surfaces, dark green text accents and one restrained sans-serif family. Use comfortable body text, generous spacing and a natural portrait or studio photograph. Let a small set of credits communicate experience without covering the page in posters.

The homepage sequence remains: identity and experience, selected credits, the existing Spotify placement, and project enquiries. The full archive provides search and filters; individual projects provide precise contributions and approved listening or viewing links. Returning to the archive should preserve the visitor's filters and place in the list.

The research supports the current structure. It does not justify adding a carousel, autoplay audio, more decorative type, or extra sections before the client supplies suitable content. The remaining editorial choices are the six featured films, accurate roles, approved listening links, a clean original portrait and optional approved testimonials.

## Review checks

- Can a visitor identify Sujith's profession and experience immediately?
- Can a producer find a relevant film and understand his exact contribution?
- Can they listen deliberately, without unexpected playback?
- Can they return from a project without losing their archive position?
- Can they make an enquiry and receive truthful success or failure feedback?
- Does the same path remain readable and usable on a narrow phone?

The current implementation has local tests for the archive and enquiry handling. Test fixtures do not represent additional real film credits. Actual enquiry delivery requires the recipient and Netlify setup described in [portfolio-setup.md](portfolio-setup.md).
